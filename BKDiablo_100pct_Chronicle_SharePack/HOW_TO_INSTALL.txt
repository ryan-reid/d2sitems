========================================================================
   BKDiablo Mod - 100% In-Game Chronicle (Holy Grail Wiki) Stash Files
========================================================================

These files provide a 100% completed in-game Chronicle tab for the BKDiablo mod in Diablo II: Resurrected.
You can use them as a full in-game wiki/reference to inspect all items, runewords, and stats!

FILES INCLUDED:
--------------
- ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i  --> Softcore shared stash (100% Chronicle, empty stash)
- ModernSharedStashHardCoreV2.d2i  --> Hardcore shared stash (100% Chronicle, empty stash)

*Note: All item container tabs in these stashes are completely blank (0 items, 0 gold) 
so your shared stash won't be cluttered with someone else's gear.

========================================================================
   INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS
========================================================================

STEP 1: Close Diablo II: Resurrected completely.

STEP 2: Open your D2R BKDiablo save folder:
   Press Windows Key + R, paste the following line, and press Enter:
   %USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\Mods\BKDiablo

STEP 3: BACK UP YOUR EXISTING SHARED STASH!
   - If playing Softcore, rename your existing "ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i" 
     to "ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i.bak".
   - If playing Hardcore, rename your existing "ModernSharedStashHardCoreV2.d2i" 
     to "ModernSharedStashHardCoreV2.d2i.bak".
   (This ensures your personal items and shared gold are never lost!)

STEP 4: Copy the file(s) from this folder into that save directory:
   - Copy ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i (for Softcore)
   - Copy ModernSharedStashHardCoreV2.d2i (for Hardcore)

STEP 5: Launch Diablo II: Resurrected, open your Shared Stash, and click 
   the "Chronicle" tab. Enjoy your 100% completed in-game wiki!

========================================================================
   HOW TO RESTORE YOUR ORIGINAL STASH
========================================================================
To switch back to your own personal stash:
1. Close D2R.
2. Go back to %USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\Mods\BKDiablo\
3. Delete or move the 100% ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i (or HardCore).
4. Rename your "ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i.bak" back to "ModernSharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i".
========================================================================
